The Stage Core Principle

Money can buy utility or amplification.
It can never buy trust, rank, or relevance.

Stage's first performers have only ever been found by people who already knew them. Every feature is audited against one question: does this make it easier for a stranger to find and trust that person?

Identity Verification ≠ Rank

A seller can confirm their identity to earn a Verified badge. That verification has zero effect on where they appear in search or discovery.

Rule §2: Trust cannot be bought

Spotlights are Gated by Sales Proof

A seller can only pay to amplify their listing (a "Spotlight") after accumulating real, receipt-confirmed sales. Money amplifies proven craft; it cannot manufacture trust from nothing.

Rule §11: Proof before amplification

0% Commission · No Payment Trap

Orders route directly to the seller's choice of WhatsApp or Telegram. Payments stay between customer and seller. Stage never acts as a tollbooth on your hustle.

Rule §12: Direct routing without lock-in

Opening Night Cold-Start Guarantee

Brand-new performers receive a guaranteed protected discovery window. Outside of algorithms, every new stage gets its spotlight to build their first audience.

Everything you
need to know.

Stage focuses on discovery, storefront identity, and order clarity. Payments stay directly between customer and seller on WhatsApp or Telegram. This avoids locking sellers into expensive payment gateway holds, lets buyers use their preferred local methods, and eliminates platform commission fees.
Every order generated through Stage's WhatsApp or Telegram routing carries a unique 4-digit receipt code (e.g. #STG-7412). When the seller confirms the transaction, this code validates the sale in Backstage, increments verified Stage Buzz stats, and unlocks Spotlight eligibility.
New performers have zero followers and zero sales on day one. Typical algorithms bury new accounts because they lack engagement data. Opening Night gives every newly launched Stage guaranteed discovery placement outside of competitive ranking for a fixed window so strangers can discover them.
Absolutely. Video and voice media are purely additive, never mandatory. The core connection mechanic is the written Opening Note. A text-only storefront is treated as 100% complete by the system, and video accounts never receive an artificial rank boost.
Stage does not force small businesses into generic platform taxonomies. A perfume creator might sell under "Arabian Mists" and "Wax Melts," while a fashion designer uses "Two Pieces" and "Baby Tops." You know your business and craft better than any generic category tree.
Displaying bare zeros ("0 reviews, 0 sales") makes honest new sellers look struggling and warns strangers away. Stage replaces bare zeros with neutral, encouraging markers ("Opening Night", "New to Stage", "Waiting for your first view") until real data is earned.
